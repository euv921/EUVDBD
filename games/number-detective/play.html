    How to play +

    Multiplayer starts with 5 points each. Each accepted guess costs 1, including a winning guess. Solving earns +5 immediately. Players at 0 sit out for the round; if everyone runs out, nobody wins. Starting the next round adds +5 to everyone’s remaining balance. Invalid or already-tried guesses do not cost points. Starting a new game from the setup screen starts a fresh competition. Solo has no point limit.

    The best record is the fewest total guesses across all players in a completed game. Each variant has its own record on this device, shared across solo, multiplayer, and supported lengths. Only a lower total replaces it. A reveal is not a solve. Switching variants keeps their puzzles and points separate.
