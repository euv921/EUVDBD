How do the clues work? +

If the secret is 123 and you guess 132, you get 1 right spot (the 1) and 2 wrong spots (the 2 and 3).

A digit is counted only once. The clues give counts, not which digits are correct. Get 3 right spots to solve the puzzle. In multiplayer, take one valid guess per turn in player order; the player who solves it wins. Invalid or already-tried guesses do not use up a turn.
