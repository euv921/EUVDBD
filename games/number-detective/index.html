    How to play +

    Find the secret code: 3 different digits from 0–9.
    A zero can come first. Play solo or take turns with 2–5 players.
    Whoever cracks the code wins.

    If the secret is 123 and you guess 132, you get 1 right spot (the 1) and 2 wrong spots (the 2 and 3).

    A digit is counted only once. The clues give counts, not which digits are correct. Get 3 right spots to solve the puzzle. In multiplayer, take one valid guess per turn in player order; the player who solves it wins. Invalid or already-tried guesses do not use up a turn or cost points.

    In multiplayer, everyone starts the competition with 5 points. Each accepted guess costs 1 point, including a correct guess. Solving earns +5 points. Players at 0 sit out for the rest of the round; if everyone runs out, there is no winner. Points accumulate: starting the next round adds +5 to everyone’s existing balance, whether or not anyone won. The winner therefore earns +10 in bonuses across the win and the next-round refill, before guess costs. Changing players starts a fresh competition at 5 each. Solo has no point limit.

    The best record is the fewest total guesses across everyone in a completed game, shared across solo and multiplayer on this device. Ties do not replace it.
