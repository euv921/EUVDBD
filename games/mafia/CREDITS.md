# Sound credits

## Witch’s Cackle recording

- Work: **Witch Cackle**
- Author: **AntumDeluge**
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/witch-cackle
- Original file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/witch_cackle-1_0.ogg
- License: **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**
- License link: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The recording is converted to mono 16 kHz PCM, embedded in `witch-sample.js`,
resampled into the game soundtrack, level-normalized, and mixed with short
room echoes. The original recording is approximately 1.16 seconds long.
It is used only in Witch’s Cackle. Other soundscapes have no laughter.

The wind, bubbles, owl-like calls, creaks, and ritual ambience are original
procedural audio generated locally. Ghosts in the Attic retains its original
samples. No audio is streamed from third-party services during gameplay.
