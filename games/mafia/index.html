A secret among you.

Your tablet is the host. Listen, close your eyes, and discover who you can trust.

Game language changes role cards, night instructions, narration, and the winner display. Bilingual presents English first, then Simplified Chinese.

Each night turn includes extra time for the spoken prompt.

1 Mafia · 1 Doctor · 2 Villagers

Discuss and vote during the day. Play until one side wins.

How to play
  1. Take turns looking at your secret role. Hide it before passing the tablet.
  2. Place the tablet in the middle. Everyone closes their eyes. Only open them when the host calls your role.
  3. The Mafia taps a living player to attack. The Doctor taps a player to protect, including themselves.
  4. At dawn, a protected target survives. In Simple mode, the game ends after one night: if anyone died, the Mafia wins; if nobody died, the Villagers win. There is no discussion or voting.
  5. With Simple mode off, discuss together during the day, then vote one player out or skip voting. The town wins when the Mafia is out. The Mafia wins when only one other player remains. Eliminated players keep their role secret and do not act.
